AETHON AI

Illuminate Threats. Uncover the Truth.

AETHON AI is an AI-powered email threat detection, forensic intelligence, and investigation platform designed to transform suspicious emails into explainable cybersecurity cases.

The platform goes beyond traditional phishing detection by analysing email content, headers, sender metadata, URLs, domains, IP addresses, attachments, and authentication signals to identify potential threats. It combines AI-driven risk analysis with rule-based detection, threat intelligence, geolocation, and forensic correlation to provide investigators with a complete view of an attack.

AETHON AI can extract and connect Indicators of Compromise (IOCs), reconstruct the observable email path, identify suspicious infrastructure, analyse lookalike domains and malicious links, and present the findings through an evidence graph and investigation timeline. The system provides an explainable risk score, helping analysts understand not only whether an email is suspicious, but why.

By correlating scattered technical indicators into a structured investigation case, AETHON AI aims to reduce manual analysis, improve threat visibility, and help security analysts move from detection to actionable forensic intelligence.

Core Capabilities

- 🤖 AI-powered phishing and threat risk detection
- 🔍 Explainable risk scoring with contributing signals
- 📧 Email header and mail-path analysis
- 🔗 URL and lookalike domain detection
- 🌐 IP intelligence and approximate geolocation
- 🧩 IOC extraction and forensic evidence correlation
- 📊 Interactive evidence graph and investigation timeline
- 📱 QR-code phishing detection and hidden URL analysis
- 🧠 AI-generated attack narratives and investigation insights
- 🔎 Cross-case similarity and phishing campaign detection
- 📄 Automated forensic investigation reports
- 🔐 Tamper-evident evidence hashing and audit trails
